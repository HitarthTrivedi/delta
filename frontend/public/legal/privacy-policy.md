# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 26 October 2026 · **Policy version:** 2026-10-26

This Privacy Policy explains how Delta collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data. It is written to comply with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** (the "DPDP Act") and the rules made under it, and, where applicable, the Information Technology Act, 2000.

We ask for your consent in the app before you can use Delta, by checkbox. We record what you agreed to and when. If we materially change this Policy, we will ask for your consent again; we never treat silence or continued use as consent.

## 3. What personal data we collect

We collect only what we need for the purposes in Section 4.

### 3.1 Account data
Your name, email address and a unique account ID. Sign-in is handled by **Supabase**, and we never see or store your password. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from Google.

### 3.2 Profile and goals
Education (institution, field of study, year), current role, target role, career goals, past experience, skills, projects, certificates, achievements, available hours, learning style, location preferences, and links you choose to add (LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio, phone).

### 3.3 Résumé data
Text and details **extracted** from a résumé you upload. We do not keep the original file after extraction.

### 3.4 Conversations
What you write to Delta's AI agents (intake, weekly planning, mock interviews), and notes derived from those conversations, so that Delta remembers your context between sessions.

### 3.5 Learning activity
Your roadmap, weekly tasks, completions, progress history and your Delta Score. For the daily habit features: the days you studied, your streak, XP and level, your daily goal and the study time you chose, your answers in the daily Quick 5 review (to decide when each question comes back), your daily quests and the badges you earn, if you join weekly leagues your league and weekly XP, if you join study circles the circles you are in, if you joined with a friend's invite link who invited you, and, if you turn reminders on, your reminder settings, the browser push address of each device you allowed, and for each reminder which wording we used and whether you studied that day (to learn which wording helps most). **XP, streaks, badges and leagues never change your test or interview scores, and recruiters never see them.** The one exception is optional: if you turn on the "Consistent learner" badge, recruiters who can already see you also see a yes/no badge when you studied on 20 or more of the last 30 days (Section 6).

### 3.6 Practice and assessment data
Your answers and results from the technical tests, the code you submit in the coding sandbox, your mock-interview transcripts, and the resulting scores, including a per-skill breakdown. For each session we also note how it was taken: whether a test was timed, how long it took, and, as counted by your browser, how often you left the tab and how much you pasted. You see these notes on your results.

If you use **Job match**, we keep the jobs you analysed: the role and the skills read from the ad, not the text you pasted. If you **share a badge**, we keep the link you created until you turn it off.

### 3.7 Consent and transparency records
What you consented to, which policy version, and when, plus a log of which recruiters opened your profile (Section 6).

### 3.8 Technical data
Login tokens kept in your browser's local or session storage, and standard server logs (such as IP address and request details) kept by our hosting providers.

### 3.9 Recruiter account data
For recruiters: name, work email, company, designation, company website, saved search preferences, and our approval decision.

### 3.10 Children's data (Delta for School)
Collected only with a parent's consent (Section 9): the child's **first name**, **class**, the **lessons** they open, and their **quiz answers and scores**. Plus a parent PIN, which we store only in hashed form. Nothing else.

### 3.12 College enrollment data
If you join through your college: the college's record of you that it uploads to Delta (roll number, name, college email, branch, section, graduation year, CGPA and backlogs, and date of birth only for under-18 students), which batch you are in, and when you joined or left.

### 3.13 College staff accounts
For placement officers and faculty: name, work email, role, and the college they manage.

### 3.14 Parents of under-18 college students
The parent's or guardian's name and email, and their consent decision (Section 9).

### 3.11 What we do not want
We don't intentionally collect financial, health, biometric, caste, religious or other sensitive data. **Please don't enter it into free-text fields or chats.** If you do, contact us and we'll remove it.

## 5. How the AI works

To answer you, Delta sends the relevant parts of your profile, messages, test answers or résumé text to an AI model hosted by **Google (Gemini API)** or **Groq**, and shows you the result. Code you run in the coding sandbox is sent to **JDoodle** to execute.

**AI mock interview.** The interviewer's questions are spoken aloud. To give the interviewer its voice and lip movement, the question text (never your answers, voice or video) is sent to **Google Cloud Text-to-Speech**. Your camera view is shown only to you, on your own screen: we never record, upload or receive your video. Your spoken answers are turned into text by **your browser's** speech recognition. In Chrome and Edge, the browser maker (Google or Microsoft) processes that audio to produce the text, under its own terms; Delta receives only the text. If you prefer, you can type your answers instead.

AI output can be wrong. Check anything important (deadlines, eligibility, salaries) against official sources. Practice scores are an indicator for your own preparation, **not a certification**.

## 6. Recruiters: only if you opt in

Recruiters **cannot see you** unless you turn on **"Visible to recruiters"** in **Privacy settings**. That is a separate, optional consent, and the rest of Delta works the same if you never switch it on.

- **Who can see you:** only recruiters whose accounts Delta has **manually verified and approved**, in one of two separate groups that never mix:
  - **Campus recruiters**: if you are on a college seat, only the recruiters your college has chosen to work with;
  - **Off-campus recruiters**: if you are not on a college seat, only recruiters hiring off-campus. They never see college students.
- **Who is never shown:** working professionals, under-18s, and college staff accounts.
- **Joining or leaving a college** switches visibility off, because the recruiters who could see you change. Switch it on again if you want to.
- **What they see:**
  - your name;
  - education (institution, field of study, year); for students on a college seat, the college, branch and graduation year as confirmed by the college;
  - target role;
  - skills and their levels;
  - practice results (technical test, coding and mock-interview scores, with a per-skill breakdown);
  - how each practice session was taken: whether it was timed, and notes such as leaving the tab or pasting code (the same notes you see on your results);
  - **only if you pass a verified readiness re-check:** "Verified readiness" with the re-check score and date, until it expires. A re-check you don't pass is **never shown** to recruiters; they just see that you aren't verified;
  - whether **their own company** asked you to take a re-check, and whether that request is open or closed;
  - your email address and any LinkedIn / GitHub / portfolio links you added, so they can contact you;
  - **only if you also turn on the optional "Consistent learner" badge:** that badge, when you studied on 20 or more of the last 30 days. It is a yes or no only.
- **What they never see:** your conversations with Delta's AI, your personal introduction/backstory, résumé text, weekly plans, progress notes, phone number, GPA, or your XP, streak, badges and league standing.
- **Verified readiness re-checks:** you can choose to take a short, strictly timed re-check (fresh questions from a shared question bank and, for tech fields, one coding problem). A recruiter who can see you may ask you to take one; you can decline. We keep which bank questions you've seen (so they aren't repeated), your re-check results and any requests, and you see all of it in your data export. A re-check is not camera or screen proctoring: we use the same timing and integrity notes as other practice sessions.
- **Transparency:** every time a recruiter opens your full profile, we log it. You can see which company viewed you, and when, in Privacy settings.
- **Recruiters' obligations:** recruiters must agree to use candidate data **only to assess candidates for roles at their own organisation**, not to copy, share or sell it, and to delete it when no longer needed. For data they take away from Delta, the recruiter is responsible as its own Data Fiduciary.
- **Withdrawing:** switch visibility off at any time. You disappear from search and profiles **immediately**. We can't recall information a recruiter already viewed before you withdrew, but they are bound to delete it when they no longer need it.

## 6A. Colleges (Delta for College)

If your college bought seats on Delta and you join with its code or your college email, you are on a **college seat**.

- **Roles.** The college decides what it uploads about its students (its roster) and how it uses the reports it sees: for that, the **college is the Data Fiduciary** and Delta processes the data for it, under a written agreement. For everything else you do on Delta, Delta is the Data Fiduciary.
- **What your college sees:** your name and roll number (from its own roster), your readiness, practice scores and per-skill results, how many days you were active and when you were last active, your progress on any plan your college assigns, and your results (with how each was taken) in tests your college sets.
- **Plans and tests your college sets.** Your college can choose a roadmap for your batch; you follow it while you are in that batch, and it stays yours (to keep or stop) if you leave. A test your college sets is the same for everyone in the batch, and its answers are shown after it closes.
- **What your college never sees:** your conversations with Delta's AI, your résumé text, job matches, study circles, leagues, XP and streaks, whether you are visible to recruiters, and who viewed you.
- **Leaving.** You can leave your college on Delta at any time (Privacy settings). Your college can also remove your seat. You keep your account as an individual learner.
- **When a college's contract ends**, its seats end. The college can export its reports for 30 days; then we delete its roster and reports. Your own account stays.
- Reports a college downloads are the college's responsibility.

## 6B. Company roadmaps

Some companies on Delta publish a **roadmap** for a role they hire for. Delta builds it from the skills the company lists, using steps written and reviewed by people, and checks every roadmap before students see it.

- **Who can see a company's roadmap:** the same groups as in Section 6. A campus company's roadmap is shown only to students of colleges that work with it; an off-campus company's roadmap only to students who aren't on a college seat. Working professionals, under-18s and college staff can't follow company roadmaps.
- **Following one is a separate consent, for that one company.** When you follow a roadmap, you agree to share with that company: your name and education (as in Section 6), your progress on that roadmap, your scores on its skills, and your results in the tests it sets (with how each was taken). You don't need to turn on "Visible to recruiters" for this, and following one roadmap shares nothing with any other company.
- **What the company never sees:** your conversations with Delta's AI, résumé text, your other plans, your email address, XP, streaks, badges, leagues or study circles.
- **Stopping:** leave the roadmap at any time (on the Company roadmaps page or in Privacy settings). The company stops seeing you **immediately**. Joining or leaving a college also ends your company roadmaps, because the companies that can see you change.
- **The company's duties** are the same as a recruiter's (Section 6 and the Terms of Use): use the data only to assess you for its own roles, don't share or sell it, and delete it when no longer needed.

## 9. Children (Delta for School and under-18 college students)

Under the DPDP Act, anyone **under 18** is a child.

- **Children cannot create accounts on their own.** Delta accounts are for people aged 18 or over, who confirm this before using Delta. The one exception, only where we have switched it on and the college allows it, is an **under-18 college student on a college seat**:
  - before the account opens, a parent or guardian gives **verifiable consent** through a one-time email link, or the college confirms it holds that consent (we record which, with the date);
  - we collect their date of birth, only to know when they turn 18;
  - their personal data is **never sent to AI providers**: no AI chat, AI intake or AI mock interviews; their plan, practice tests and coding problems work without it;
  - **no recruiters, leagues, study circles, invite links or public badge links**, and reminders use one fixed wording;
  - when they turn 18 they give their own consent, and only then can they choose recruiter visibility;
  - a parent can withdraw consent by writing to us; the account is then deleted.
- **Parent-managed profiles.** A parent or legal guardian, signed in to their own adult account, may add a child profile for Delta for School. Before we process anything about the child, the parent must confirm they are the child's parent or guardian and give **explicit consent**. We record that consent with its date and policy version.
- **How the child uses Delta.** The child learns inside the parent's account in a restricted "child mode" that shows only their lessons. Leaving it needs the parent's PIN. The child has no login, email or contact details of their own on Delta.
- **What we process:** only what Section 3.10 lists, and only to show lessons and progress (Section 4).
- **What we never do with children's data:**
  - no tracking, behavioural monitoring, profiling or targeted advertising;
  - no sending it to AI providers;
  - no showing it to recruiters;
  - no AI chat with children.
- **Content.** Lessons follow the NCERT syllabus. They are drafted with AI and then **reviewed and approved by a person** before any child sees them.
- **Parent control.** In Delta for School, the parent can see the child's progress, and can **remove the child at any time**. That withdraws consent and immediately erases the child's profile and progress. Deleting the parent's account also erases every child profile on it.
- **A note on verification.** We rely on the parent being a signed-in adult who has confirmed their age and their relationship to the child. If we learn that a child profile was created without a genuine parent's consent, we will delete it. Parents or guardians can contact [hitartht318@gmail.com](mailto:hitartht318@gmail.com) at any time.

## 10. How long we keep data

- We keep your data **while your account is active**.
- When you **delete your account** (which also withdraws your consent), we erase your personal data **immediately** from our database. Copies in our providers' backups expire on their normal cycles.
- **Backups.** We take an encrypted backup of the database every night and keep each one for **30 days**, after which it is deleted automatically. So data you delete disappears from every backup within 30 days. Backups are used only to recover from data loss.
- **College seats** end when you leave, when your college removes you, or when its contract ends; the college's roster and reports are deleted 30 days after its contract ends.
- We keep a **minimal consent record** (account ID, what was consented to or withdrawn, policy version, date) after deletion. It contains no name or email, and is kept as proof of consent as the law requires.
- Security and access logs are kept for as long as applicable law requires.
- Aggregated or de-identified data that can no longer identify you may be kept.

## 11. Security

- Verified sign-in tokens on every request.
- Ownership checks so no user can read another's data.

- Rate limiting.
- Access to production data limited to the Operator.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your password private.
